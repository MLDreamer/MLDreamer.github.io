About

I don't believe in AI magic.

I believe in the mathematics. That isn't a tagline — it's a filter. It tells me which problems are worth solving, which architectures will survive production, and which AI initiatives are theatre dressed as transformation.

Architecture decisions I've owned in production:

  • Zero-shot vs. per-series training: Chose cross-learning TFT so new SKUs were forecastable on day one without retraining — 90+ markets, 25K+ SKUs
  • Retrieval-grounded LLM vs. fine-tuned: Insisted on RAG + deterministic validation layer before any figure reached a planner — zero hallucinated numbers in production
  • State-aware async agent runtime: Designed checkpointing + self-recovery so agent fleets fail gracefully, not catastrophically
  • Decoupled from vendor runtime: A2A + MCP abstraction layer — same workflow runs on Gemini, Bedrock, Vertex AI or Azure OpenAI without rewriting agent logic
  • Governance as first-class citizen: Trust interceptors, RBAC, HITL escalation designed into the runtime — not added after the compliance team asks

How I got here

I came up through mathematics. A Boeing-sponsored PhD from IIT Bombay in Optimization & Operations Research — stochastic programming, large-scale combinatorial optimization, simulation under uncertainty — gave me something rare in this field: the ability to prove what I build, not just demo it. Before that, an M.Tech from NIT Tiruchirappalli and a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from WBUT.

Then quantitative finance — WorldQuant and a Geneva family office — where feedback is instant and brutal. A wrong model costs money the same day. It installed an evaluation discipline I never lost. Then production ML for a US credit card company, healthcare NLP for CVS Health inside a HIPAA environment, and nearly four years at Unilever.

At Unilever I didn't join an existing AI team. I built the entire forecasting and decision-intelligence function from scratch — hiring the 30-person org, defining the architecture, choosing the model strategy, managing P&L, and governing three Big 4 firms (EY, Deloitte, BCG) concurrently. The results — 4–5pp accuracy step-change, £2.5M+ revenue uplift, 20% fewer stockouts, 200+ daily users across 90+ markets — came from the mathematics. Not the slide deck.

Now at Cognizant I work on what comes after the model. I architect enterprise agentic AI platforms — autonomous agent fleets that are observable, self-healing and governed for GDPR / HIPAA / EU AI Act — including the semantic ontologies that let Google Gemini agents reason consistently across heterogeneous enterprise data, with multi-cloud orchestration across AWS Bedrock, GCP Vertex AI and Azure OpenAI.

What I care about

Most teams building agents are solving for capability. I'm solving for industrialisation. Trust and evaluation are the product, not an afterthought. The hard part is not the model — it's proving, to a regulator and a skeptic, when a system is right, when it is unsure, and when it should stop and ask a human.

I am wary of fluent chain-of-thought that only reads like reasoning. I design for the moment a model is confidently wrong — deterministic guardrails, human-in-the-loop escalation, audit trails, and evals that reflect the real distribution, not a curated demo. This is what governance-by-design means in practice.

What I've shipped and how

Zero-shot forecasting platform

TFT cross-learning model so new markets and SKUs are forecastable without retraining. Databricks feature store, MLflow, Airflow orchestration, Bayesian safety-stock, real-time inference into SAP IBP. Built end-to-end from scratch at Unilever — £2.5M+ uplift.

RAG-grounded LLM co-pilot

Hybrid retrieval + cross-encoder reranking, deterministic validation guardrail before any output reaches a user, HITL approval for plan actions. 200+ planners daily, zero hallucinated figures. The change management was the hard part, not the model.

Production agentic platform

Semantic ontologies for Gemini agent reasoning across heterogeneous enterprise data. A2A + MCP abstraction makes the workflow cloud-portable. State-aware async runtime with checkpointing, RBAC, HITL escalation and full audit trails.

Clinical NLP pipeline

BERT/GPT NER + ICD-10 classification inside a HIPAA environment. AUCPR-validated from the start, full audit trail per prediction, human coder always able to trace a decision back to source text. −35% claims processing time at CVS Health.
